# MINXIN context and Major Concerns

## School planning lens

Use these identifiers at unit level. They guide choices but are not substitutes for subject outcomes.

- `MC1 — Global engagement and future readiness`: deepen meaningful partnerships; use appropriate AI-driven teaching, innovation, or smart-campus contexts; build students' AI literacy and global engagement.
- `MC2 — Academic excellence and pedagogical innovation`: apply research-informed subject pedagogy, interdisciplinary collaboration where it strengthens the subject, evidence-driven revision, and continuous improvement.
- `MC3 — Holistic development and global outlook`: develop Values Education, ATL/generic skills, self-management, collaboration, rotating leadership, handover, public communication, and informed global perspectives.

For each mapped concern, record a short `major_concern_evidence` statement in `Units`, such as “students compare two regional data sets and justify source reliability” or “group roles rotate and each learner submits an individual evidence note”. Do not accept a label without observable student action or evidence.

## Formal SOW language

- Use concise British English by default.
- Make objectives detailed and assessable; keep Values / National Education to one short phrase.
- Write activities as a purposeful sequence rather than a list of fashionable strategies.
- After assessment, use subject-appropriate analysis, correction, re-practice, revision, reflection, and target adjustment.
- On short weeks, prefer consolidation, feedback, a bounded milestone, or transition.

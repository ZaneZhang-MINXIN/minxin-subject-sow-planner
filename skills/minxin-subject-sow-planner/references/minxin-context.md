Use these official identifiers as a planning lens. They guide choices but are not substitutes for subject outcomes.

Use `MC1`, `MC2`, and `MC3` as workbook reference codes and display them as `M1`, `M2`, and `M3` in the Word SOW. Leave `Weekly_Plan.major_concern_refs` blank by default. Fill it only where the linked objective has a direct, evidence-based relationship to the concern. The generated marker belongs in Learning Objectives, not Values and not every row. The complete official wording must appear once in `Remarks: Major Concerns (2025–2028)` after the main table, outside all tables.

## Formal SOW language

- Use concise British English by default.
- Make objectives detailed and assessable; keep Values / National Education to one short phrase.
- Write activities as a purposeful sequence rather than a list of fashionable strategies.
- After assessment, use subject-appropriate analysis, correction, re-practice, revision, reflection, and target adjustment.
- On short weeks, prefer consolidation, feedback, a bounded milestone, or transition.
